Data Protection Overview

RubyBug LLC operates RubyBug Apps under strict data minimization standards. We never sell, rent, or monetize merchant data. Platform integration connects solely via official tokenized OAuth 2.0 authorization codes approved directly by the seller.

1. Company Entity & Purpose

This Privacy Policy governs the operations of RubyBug Apps, a multi-store eCommerce operational platform provided by RubyBug LLC ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), with registered principal address at 1001 S MAIN ST, STE 600, KALISPELL, MT 59901-1498.

RubyBug Apps helps authorized eCommerce merchants consolidate cross-channel order data, manage store operations, and monitor delivery logistics across supported marketplace platforms (such as TikTok Shop, Shopify, Amazon SP).

2. Scope of Data Collection

We restrict data collection exclusively to what is technically necessary to execute merchant-directed operations:

  • Authorization Data: Scoped OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens granted through marketplace partner consent flows. We never ask for, view, or store marketplace master account passwords.
  • Store & Catalog Metadata: Merchant store identifiers, seller IDs, and SKU synchronization mapping data.
  • Transactional Order Data: Order reference IDs, buyer name and shipping address needed to fulfil the order (masked in the interface and encrypted at rest), item quantities, fulfillment statuses, and courier tracking numbers required for inventory and fulfillment sync.
  • Voluntary Correspondence: Inquiries sent directly to our support inbox (aissatalyah524@hotmail.com).

Exclusions: We do not collect customer credit card numbers, payment banking details, or unapproved biometric/location data. The public marketing website does not employ third-party advertising tracking cookies or invasive pixels.

3. How We Use Information

Merchant data is used strictly to fulfill the software capabilities requested by the account administrator:

  • Facilitating authorized API synchronization between seller channels.
  • Detecting inventory discrepancies and consolidating order fulfillment workflows.
  • Responding to customer service, technical diagnostics, and compliance inquiries.
  • Fulfilling legal obligations and maintaining security audit logs.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

All information processed by RubyBug LLC is protected under stringent security baselines. In transit, all network requests enforce HTTPS using TLS 1.2 or higher. At rest, database records and access tokens are secured using industry-standard AES-256 cryptographic encryption. For detailed technical policies, visit our Security Policy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

We retain operational data only as long as an active authorization exists between the merchant's store and RubyBug Apps.

  • Token Revocation / App Uninstallation: When a merchant uninstalls or revokes authorization through the marketplace partner portal (such as TikTok Shop Partner Center), active access tokens are immediately invalidated. All associated cached synchronization records are permanently deleted within 48 hours.
  • Manual Erasure Requests: Merchants may request the immediate, permanent deletion of their account history at any time. We fulfill requests within 30 days. Full procedures are available in our Data Deletion Policy.

6. Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

RubyBug LLC does not sell, trade, or monetize merchant data under any circumstances. We share information only with:

  • The specific marketplace platform APIs (e.g. TikTok Shop Open API) authorized by the merchant to execute order updates.
  • Certified infrastructure service providers (cloud hosting, encrypted storage) bound by strict non-disclosure and security agreements.
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities solely when mandated by valid legal process.

7. Security Incident Management

We maintain an active security monitoring program. In the unlikely event of a security breach affecting customer or partner data, RubyBug LLC commits to notifying platform partners (including TikTok Shop) and affected merchants within 72 hours. Security researchers and users may report vulnerabilities via our Incident Reporting Channel.

8. Merchant & Data Subject Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, merchants and individuals enjoy the right to access, rectify, restrict, or request the deletion of their personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at aissatalyah524@hotmail.com.
