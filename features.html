Architectural Capabilities • In Design Preview

Everything You Need for
Multi-Store Clarity.

Explore how RubyBug Apps is architected to eliminate multi-tab fragmentation, unify cross-channel order data, and keep daily eCommerce operations in complete harmony.

Product status: The sections on this page describe capabilities being designed. None of them is offered as a live connection, live sync, or live order console on this website.
01 • Workspace

Multi-Store
Fleet Management

A merchant who sells through more than one account usually keeps a separate login, catalog context, and order list for each store. RubyBug Apps is designed to place those authorized accounts in one workspace so you can see which stores are connected without opening each seller environment in turn.

The Merchant Challenge

The first hour of the day disappears into repeated logins across 5+ marketplace tabs. The work is not difficult, but it is duplicated for every store you run.

Operational Benefit

Connected accounts appear as a unified roster. Determine instantly which accounts are authorized and active before opening any order details.

Limitation: A store appears only after that merchant authorizes that account. RubyBug Apps does not discover un-authorized stores.

02 • Orders

Centralized
Order Management

Order detail that stays isolated inside each platform forces merchants to rebuild daily working lists by hand. The order view in RubyBug Apps is architected as one table: supported order records, the store each record belongs to, and a unified status you can scan in seconds.

The Merchant Challenge

“What still needs attention today?” has no single answer when each store keeps its own siloed queue.

Operational Benefit

Review one consolidated pipeline instead of piecing together spreadsheets. The store name stays attached so an order is never detached from its source.

Limitation: Displays only fields provided by authorized integrations. RubyBug Apps does not replace marketplace fulfillment tools.

03 • Data Movement

Order Data
Synchronization

Copying order numbers, statuses, and tracking codes from one window to another is prone to expensive human error. Synchronization is the automated alternative: after authorization, supported order data streams through official platform APIs into your unified workspace.

The Merchant Challenge

Manual copy-pasting creates duplicate records that drift out of sync as soon as a customer changes an address or cancels an item.

Operational Benefit

The workspace receives authoritative updates automatically, eliminating retyping and status drift.

Limitation: Synchronization reflects only data permitted by the platform API. Live sync is currently in development.

04 • Permission & Security

Authorized
Account Connections

Sharing a store password with a tool is an unacceptable security vulnerability. RubyBug Apps is architected strictly around official OAuth 2.0 authorization: the merchant approves requested scopes directly on the marketplace portal and can revoke access anytime.

The Merchant Challenge

Sellers need to connect channels without handing over master credentials or granting unreviewed account privileges.

Operational Benefit

Zero password storage. You maintain 100% control over granted permissions, with tokens isolated per tenant.

Limitation: Connection flows are offered only where official platform APIs and partner approvals are active.

05 • Operational Summary

Centralized
Operational Visibility

Checking stores separately never provides a coherent picture of total business velocity. The overview provides a high-level operational pulse: active connections, orders currently in view, and urgent alerts requiring your team's immediate touch.

The Merchant Challenge

Without a shared summary, staff must cycle through every store dashboard before identifying which orders are late.

Operational Benefit

Your workday starts from a single screen that points directly to orders needing fulfillment, saving hours every week.

Limitation: Numbers reflect data ingested through APIs; they are not marketplace sales analytics.
