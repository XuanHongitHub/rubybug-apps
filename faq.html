General & Product Scope

RubyBug Apps is an eCommerce management platform developed by RubyBug LLC. It is designed to help online merchants organize multiple seller stores, bring supported order information into one workspace, and simplify daily store operations.
RubyBug Apps is designed for online merchants, multi-store sellers, and eCommerce businesses that need a clearer way to organize more than one seller account without repetitive browser logins.
Multi-store management means keeping more than one authorized seller account in a single workspace, instead of treating each store as a completely separate daily routine.

Connection & Synchronization

The product is designed so a merchant can authorize more than one seller account. Each connection depends on that merchant approving the requested permissions. Connecting multiple accounts is part of the product design and is not offered as a live connection on this website.
Supported order information from connected accounts is intended to be listed in one workspace, with the store identified on each record. Only information made available through an authorized integration can appear.
After a merchant authorizes a supported integration, RubyBug Apps is designed to read the order information that integration permits and bring it into the workspace. Synchronization is being developed and is not a live service on this website.

Security & Authorization

The merchant starts the connection, reviews the permissions presented by the supported authorization process, and chooses whether to approve them. The authorization flow is being developed and is not presented as live.
Access is strictly limited to the information a merchant approves through a supported authorization flow, and to what that platform API actually provides under that grant.
Authorization is required before RubyBug Apps can retrieve information from a seller account. Visiting this website does not require an account, a password, or a subscription.
This website is public. Availability of a specific integration depends on implementation, platform approval, the merchant's account eligibility, and the permissions that merchant grants.
