Privacy Policy.
RubyBug Apps.

Draft for company review. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Notice of Policy Scope: This policy describes how RubyBug LLC handles information in connection with the public RubyBug Apps website and the software architecture in design. It states solely what this website currently supports and does not claim certifications, third-party vendor subprocessors, or storage schedules until formally approved.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how RubyBug LLC handles information in connection with the public RubyBug Apps website at https://rubybugllc.com and the RubyBug Apps product described on that site. The product is an eCommerce management platform being designed to help merchants organize authorized seller accounts and supported order information.

The website is informational. It does not require an account, a password, or a subscription to browse. Visiting this public site does not initiate or require any seller-account connection.

2. Company Information

RubyBug Apps is a software product of RubyBug LLC. Principal address: 1001 S MAIN ST, STE 600, KALISPELL, MT 59901-1498. Privacy inquiries and data governance questions may be directed to aissatalyah524@hotmail.com.

3. Information We May Collect

This static website does not operate an account database and does not provide an interactive form that saves data on RubyBug servers. Information the company may receive is limited strictly to what a person chooses to transmit voluntarily—for example, sending an email to our official contact inbox—and data a future authorized platform integration is explicitly approved to ingest.

This policy confirms that the website does not utilize analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or invasive tag managers.

4. Information Provided Voluntarily

When using the contact page, the browser opens the local device's native mail client and prepares a message draft addressed to aissatalyah524@hotmail.com. That communication is delivered only when the sender approves dispatch from their email application. The static website does not retain an intermediary copy.

5. Account and Authorization Information

RubyBug Apps is architected so that any future platform connection originates via the merchant’s official authorization on a supported marketplace platform (OAuth 2.0). Until that connector is deployed and approved, this website does not collect seller credentials, API keys, access tokens, or buyer order records.

The product never prompts merchants to provide marketplace master passwords as a substitute for official tokenized authorization protocols.

6. Data Storage and Retention

General email correspondence is stored within our secure business email hosting to respond to commercial and product inquiries. Once account connection services become active, token retention will strictly follow marketplace developer policies, ensuring immediate deletion upon token revocation.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Merchants and correspondents may request the deletion of any voluntarily submitted contact information or inquiry history by emailing aissatalyah524@hotmail.com. Requests will be acknowledged and processed promptly.
