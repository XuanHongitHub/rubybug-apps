Incident Response SLA

RubyBug LLC treats platform stability, data confidentiality, and API integrity with top priority. We operate an active security response protocol to triage, remediate, and report incidents rapidly.

Initial Acknowledgment: Within 24 Hours
Breach Notification SLA: Within 72 Hours
Status Updates: Every 48h until resolved

Responsible Disclosure Policy

If you believe you have discovered a potential security vulnerability in RubyBug Apps, our API endpoints, or our public infrastructure, we encourage you to notify us promptly.

  • Provide full details and steps to reproduce (proof of concept).
  • Do not exploit the vulnerability to view, alter, or destroy data belonging to other merchants.
  • Give RubyBug LLC reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure.
