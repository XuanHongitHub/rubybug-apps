Data Deletion Policy.
Instructions & SLA.

Applicable to all RubyBug Apps integrations • Updated November 18, 2026

Right to Erasure Commitment

In compliance with global data privacy frameworks (GDPR Article 17, CCPA/CPRA) and marketplace partner developer agreements (including TikTok Shop Open API Data Protection Standards), RubyBug LLC provides clear, automated, and manual mechanisms to completely erase all merchant and customer data upon request or deauthorization.

1. Automatic Deletion upon App Uninstallation

When a merchant disconnects, uninstalls, or revokes permissions for RubyBug Apps inside their marketplace seller portal (e.g. TikTok Shop Seller Center > My Apps > Unauthorize):

01 • Instant Token Invalidation

All OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are immediately revoked and rendered inoperable.

02 • 48 Hours Database Purge

All synchronized store caches, fulfillment queues, and mapping tables are permanently removed from database storage.

03 • 30 Days Backup Rotation

Encrypted transactional backup snapshots expire and are overwritten automatically.

2. Webhook & Automated Marketplace Callbacks

RubyBug Apps actively listens for official platform deauthorization webhooks (such as TikTok Shop Data Deletion Webhooks / Deauthorization Callbacks). Upon receipt of an automated platform erasure payload, our system executes automated cryptographic shredding of the corresponding merchant record without requiring manual merchant action.

3. Manual Data Deletion Instructions

Merchants or authorized account holders may submit a manual data deletion request at any time by following these simple steps:

1
Send Written Request

Send an email from your registered account email address to aissatalyah524@hotmail.com with the subject line: DATA DELETION REQUEST - [Your Store Name / Seller ID].

2
Identity & Account Verification

Within 24 business hours, our privacy team will acknowledge receipt and verify store ownership to protect against unauthorized deletion tampering.

3
Execution & Deletion Certificate

All associated records, access tokens, and transactional metadata will be irreversibly erased within 30 calendar days. We issue a formal written confirmation email once the process is complete.

4. Scope of Data Subject to Deletion

Data Category Specific Elements Action Taken
OAuth Credentials Access tokens, refresh tokens, seller authorization scopes Permanently purged immediately
Store Metadata Store identifier, seller profile name, channel identifiers Deleted within 48 hours
Order Sync Caches Order references, SKU mappings, sync timestamps Irreversibly shredded
Support Correspondence Helpdesk messages, inquiry logs, communication history Erased unless mandated by statutory audit law

5. Statutory Retention Exceptions

In narrow circumstances, certain records may be retained temporarily solely to comply with federal or state statutory obligations (such as tax records, anti-fraud compliance, or legal disputes). Such retained records are quarantined, encrypted, and isolated from all operational query paths until the statutory retention window expires.
